Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask on WhatsApp.
Is my date still open?

Send your date through the form or on WhatsApp. We'll reply to let you know if it's available.

I don't want to look overdone. Can it stay natural?

Yes. Our style is light and glowing — makeup our clients describe as natural and delicate. You'll talk through the look together first.

Can you get a group ready?

Yes. Tell us how many people need hair or makeup and we'll confirm what fits your timeline.

Do you only do weddings?

No. We do makeup, hair and brows for events, photo shoots and everyday care too. Bridal is one of four services.
